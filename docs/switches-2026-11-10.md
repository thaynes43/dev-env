Short names below: operator `operator/app/helmrelease.yaml`, keeper `keeper/app/helmrelease.yaml`, broker `broker/app/helmrelease.yaml`, portal config `portal/app/config.yaml`, portal deploy `portal/app/deployment.yaml`. An absent boolean is off when its default is false. Those operator defaults are the sentence at operator helm line 473.

"Needs Tom" means the next step waits for him. "Does not need Tom" means a later worker can do the call without asking. A switch that starts sessions, grants access, deletes a home or pages him is not turned on from this sheet.
